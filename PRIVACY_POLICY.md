# Winner Spin Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Winner Spin is an entertainment and portfolio game. It does not offer
real-money gambling, betting, cash prizes, or withdrawals. This Privacy Policy
explains what information Winner Spin processes, why it is processed, and the
choices available to users.

## Information We Process

### Account information

When you create or use an account, Winner Spin processes:

- your email address;
- your chosen username;
- a Firebase-generated user identifier;
- email verification status; and
- password-reset request timestamps used to enforce request limits.

Passwords and authentication credentials are processed by Google Firebase
Authentication. Winner Spin does not store plaintext passwords.

### Profile and gameplay information

Winner Spin stores information needed to provide and synchronize the game,
including:

- your selected profile avatar;
- virtual coin balance and last virtual win;
- free-spin status and accumulated virtual rewards;
- shared virtual pool state; and
- account creation and gameplay-related timestamps.

Coins, spins, bonuses, and rewards are entirely virtual and have no real-world
monetary value.

### Information stored on your device

The app stores limited data locally on your device, such as:

- recent game history;
- pending-spin recovery information;
- music preferences; and
- whether the first-launch notice has been shown.

This local information is used only to operate the app and restore its state.
It can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

### Security and technical information

On Android, Firebase App Check with Play Integrity may process device and app
integrity signals to help protect the backend from abuse. Network requests may
also involve standard technical information such as IP address, device type,
operating-system information, and request timestamps.

Winner Spin requires an active internet connection. While the app is in use,
it periodically checks Google-hosted endpoints to determine whether real
internet access is available and reacts to changes in the device's active
connection type, such as Wi-Fi or mobile data. These checks do not include your
Winner Spin account content, but the endpoint provider may receive standard
network information such as your IP address and request timestamp.

## How We Use Information

Winner Spin uses information to:

- create, authenticate, verify, and manage accounts;
- save and synchronize profile and gameplay state;
- send verification and password-reset emails;
- recover interrupted game sessions;
- provide account deletion;
- protect the app and backend against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
  and
- diagnose and maintain essential app functionality.

Winner Spin does not sell personal information. The app does not include
advertising SDKs or behavioral advertising.

## Service Providers and External Links

Winner Spin uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication,
Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Firebase App Check. Google may process
information as a service provider under its own terms and privacy
documentation.

The app may open external links to GitHub and Apple. Once opened, those
services process information under their own privacy policies.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Retention and Deletion

Cloud account and gameplay information is retained while your account remains
active or as needed to provide and secure the service. You can use
**My Profile > Delete Account** to request deletion of your Firebase
Authentication account and associated Winner Spin cloud records.

Deleting your account also removes the Winner Spin files stored on your device
for that account, including your game history.

**One record is kept after deletion.** When you first open the game you are
asked to accept a disclaimer confirming, among other things, that you are 18 or
older and that the game offers no real-money gambling. Before your account is
removed we keep a copy of that acceptance: your email address, the version of
the text you accepted, when you accepted it, and the app version you used. We
keep it to be able to establish and defend legal claims about the period you
used the service, and for no other purpose. It is not used to contact you, is
never shared for marketing, and cannot be read or changed from the app.

Limited records may remain temporarily in provider backups or logs where
required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or technical
recovery. Information stored locally on your device outside the accounts you
have used may remain until the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

## Your Choices

You can:

- view your account email and profile information in the app;
- change your profile avatar;
- request a password-reset email;
- sign out; and
- delete your account from the My Profile screen.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, service providers, or legal
requirements change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in the
[Winner Spin GitHub repository](https://github.com/Winner-Spin/WinnerSpin/issues).
