# Manual Disclaimer Acceptance Retention

This document explains the public retention commitment for restricted
disclaimer-acceptance records kept after account deletion.

- Records are retained for no longer than five years after archive.
- Retention review and deletion are manual, not automatic.
- An authorized administrative review is performed at least once every three
  months.
- Records become eligible for deletion four years and six months after archive
  and are deleted after authorized independent verification.
- Missing or malformed archive values are reviewed separately and are never
  assumed eligible for deletion.
- Only high-level, PII-free evidence of completed reviews and deletion outcomes
  is retained.

Verified earlier-deletion requests may be sent to
`winnerspinapp@gmail.com` and are handled manually.
